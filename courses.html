AutoCAD Complete

2D + 3D and rendering

₹6,00024 hrs · 12 days

What you will learn

  • Precision drawing with coordinate systems, OSNAP and polar tracking
  • Trim, extend, offset, array and stretch

  • Annotation, dimensioning and GD&T-ready drawings

  11. 11Advanced 3D Editing & Visualization

ANSYS Complete

APDL + Workbench + CFD

₹12,00036 hrs · 18 days

What you will learn

  • Classical FEA with command-based APDL
  • Structural, thermal, modal and dynamic simulation in Workbench
  • Rigid-body and explicit dynamics (impact and drop test)
  • CFD: pipe flow, turbine blade, battery pack cooling, aerofoil, car aerodynamics
  • Meshing, convergence and post-processing
  • Report writing and presentation

Tools

ANSYS APDL, Workbench, Fluent (CFD), SpaceClaim / Design Modeler

Best suited for

Design engineers who need to validate parts under load, heat, vibration or flow.

18-day roadmap

  1. 01Fundamentals of FEA & 1D Beam Analysis
  2. 021D Truss Structure Analysis
  3. 032D Plane Stress/Strain Analysis
  4. 04Thermal Analysis of Composite Wall
  5. 05Static & Transient Structural
  6. 06Steady-State & Transient Thermal
  7. 07Modal Analysis & Eigenvalue Buckling
  8. 08Rigid Body Dynamics
  9. 09Explicit Dynamics (Impact / Drop Test)
  10. 10Internal Pipe Flow (CFD)
  11. 11Turbine Blade Flow
  12. 12Battery Pack Thermal Management
  13. 13Aerofoil (NACA) Aerodynamics
  14. 14Car Aerodynamics & Drag
  15. 15Capstone: Project Selection & Problem Definition
  16. 16Capstone: Modeling & Meshing
  17. 17Capstone: Setup & Solving
  18. 18Capstone: Results, Report & Presentation
Capstone. Individual end-to-end simulation such as a heavy-duty brake disc, heat exchanger, EV battery pack thermal study, aerofoil or wind turbine blade, or automotive crash.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 August 2026

1. Introduction

Nishlab Creations ("we", "us", "our") is an MSME (Udyam) registered online engineering software training institute based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect the personal information of visitors, enquirers and enrolled students who use our website, courses and related services.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website, submit an enquiry, register for a free demo, or enroll in a course, we may collect:

  • Full name, phone number and email address
  • Educational background and course interest
  • Payment and enrollment details (processed securely; we do not store full card/bank details)
  • Messages or notes you submit through our contact and enquiry forms
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, for improving site performance

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to enquiries and course-related questions
  • Register you for free demo lectures or workshops
  • Process enrollments, batch scheduling and certificate issuance
  • Send updates about batches, schedules and relevant course information
  • Improve our courses, website and student support

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers strictly for operational purposes (such as payment processing or scheduling tools), and only to the extent necessary to deliver our services.

5. Data Storage & Security

Enquiry and registration data submitted through our website forms is stored securely and accessed only by authorized Nishlab Creations team members. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies or browser storage to remember preferences and improve your browsing experience. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at nishad@nishlabcreations.com or call +91 84240 99617.

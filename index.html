How a course runs

From first sketch to first interview

The path is built in order. Each stage gives you something to put in your portfolio.

  1. STAGE 01

    Design

    AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA V5, Creo and Fusion 360. Parts, assemblies and drawings.

  2. STAGE 02

    Simulate

    ANSYS APDL, Workbench and CFD. Prove the part works before it is built.

  3. STAGE 03

    Present

    KeyShot renders and animation that make your work look professional.

  4. STAGE 04

    Automate

    Python and Excel/VBA to remove repeated work and build tools.

  5. STAGE 05

    Get hired

    ATS resume, LinkedIn, portfolio and mock technical and HR interviews.
